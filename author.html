Background

Uzi Arslan founded PrimeStar Wash in Houston, Texas in 2022 after identifying a consistent problem in the local market: exterior cleaning companies that either used incorrect techniques (high pressure on surfaces that require soft washing), lacked proper insurance, or charged for work that didn't match what was delivered. PrimeStar Wash was built to close that gap — focused on technique accuracy, transparency, and a genuine understanding of Houston's unique climate challenges.

Based at 7600 Highmeadow Dr inside Beltway 8 near the Energy Corridor, the business serves all of Houston's residential neighborhoods and the Greater Houston metro including Sugar Land, Katy, Pearland, and Cypress. Uzi personally leads or directly supervises every job — PrimeStar Wash does not subcontract work.

Expertise and Credentials

  • Soft washing and pressure washing — trained in ARMA-approved roof cleaning techniques, surface-appropriate pressure calibration, and biodegradable detergent formulation
  • Licensed, bonded, and insured — fully licensed in Texas, bonded, carrying general liability insurance and workers' compensation; proof of insurance available on request before any job
  • 500+ completed residential jobs — across Houston's most diverse housing stock, from 1950s brick bungalows in the Heights to 2020s stucco builds in Bridgeland
  • 5.0 average rating — maintained across 500+ customer reviews on Google and other platforms
  • Houston climate knowledge — direct experience with the specific biological growth patterns, soil types, and maintenance cycles that apply to Gulf Coast homes
