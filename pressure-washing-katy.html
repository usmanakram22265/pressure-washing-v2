Local to Katy

Family-first cleaning for Katy's master-planned neighborhoods.

Katy ISD sets the bar for the suburbs west of Houston. Families move here for the schools, the parks, and the kind of neighborhoods where kids ride bikes to a friend's house. Cinco Ranch, Cross Creek Ranch, Firethorne, and Falcon Ranch all share something else too — strict HOA standards on exterior maintenance and a lot of kids and pets running around backyards.

That's why every PrimeStar Wash visit to Katy starts with a landscape pre-rinse: we soak every plant, bush, and patch of grass with fresh water before any detergent touches your house. Once the wash is done, we post-rinse again. Cleaning solutions break down to inert components within hours and never linger on grass or paving.

We dispatch from Houston via I-10 West (about 25 minutes outside rush hour). Quotes are free, returned in 24 hours, and never come with a sales pitch.
