Why Houston is different

Houston's climate is the reason exterior cleaning matters.

Houston averages 50 inches of rainfall per year and maintains 70–90% relative humidity for most of the calendar. That moisture level is enough to sustain mold, mildew, and algae growth on virtually any exterior surface that stays damp for more than 24 hours — which, in Houston, means almost everything.

The most visible sign is Gloeocapsa magma, the dark-streaking cyanobacteria that spreads across asphalt roof shingles. It's present in nearly every Houston neighborhood and spreads by wind from untreated roofs to neighboring homes. Left unaddressed, it traps moisture against shingles, accelerates granule loss, and cuts years off a roof's effective life.

Concrete driveways and patios in Houston develop black algae in low-drainage zones within months of installation. Tree canopy — the live oaks and pecans that define Houston's residential character — deposits tannin-rich leaf matter and sap that permanently stains unsealed surfaces if left through one rain season.

The solution isn't more frequent cleaning — it's using the right technique the first time. Soft washing with biodegradable detergent kills biological growth at the root, which delays recolonization significantly compared to pressure-only washing. This is why Houston homes that are cleaned professionally stay clean longer than homes DIY-washed with a rented machine.

Common questions

Houston pressure washing FAQ.

How much does pressure washing cost in Houston? expand_more

Every job is different, so we quote each one individually. Variables include surface size, staining level, surface type, and any add-on services. Request a free quote online or by phone and we'll return a written price within 24 hours — with no pressure to accept.

How often should a Houston home be pressure washed? expand_more

In Houston's climate, house exteriors benefit from washing every 12–18 months. Driveways every 6–12 months depending on tree coverage and vehicle traffic. Roofs every 2–3 years when soft-washed correctly. Fences annually if wood, every 1–2 years if vinyl.

Do I need to be home during the job? expand_more

No. We need access to an exterior water spigot and the surfaces being cleaned. You don't need to be present. We contact you when we arrive and when we finish, and payment is due on completion — by phone or in person, whichever works for you.

Is soft washing safe for my landscaping and pets? expand_more

Yes. We use biodegradable detergents and pre-rinse all landscaping, garden beds, and lawn areas before applying any solution. Post-rinse is standard on every job. Our products are formulated to be safe around plants and pets once rinsed and dried. We've worked in landscaped Houston properties for years without incident.

Will pressure washing damage my roof shingles? expand_more

Not with our method. We use the ARMA-approved soft wash technique — applying cleaning solution at under 100 PSI at the shingle surface. This does not dislodge granules or void manufacturer warranties. High-pressure washing on shingles IS damaging, which is why professional roof cleaners don't use it.
