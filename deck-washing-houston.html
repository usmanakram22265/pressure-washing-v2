Deck Washing in Houston

Why Houston homes choose PrimeStar for deck washing.

Houston decks — particularly in Memorial, Heights, West University, and River Oaks — are heavily used outdoor living spaces that bear the full brunt of the Gulf Coast climate year-round. Houston's combination of year-round humidity, frequent rain, and tree coverage from live oaks and magnolias means wood decks develop gray fiber damage and mildew within one season. Composite and Trex decking develops tannin staining and algae faster than most homeowners expect in the Greater Houston climate.

We soft-wash composite and Trex decking at low pressure with a biodegradable cleaner safe for manufacturer warranties. Wood decks get a medium-pressure wash plus an optional wood brightener (oxalic acid rinse) that restores the original color before any stain or sealer is applied. All surrounding landscaping is pre-rinsed before any solution contact.

Common questions

Deck Washing FAQ — Houston.

Is deck washing safe for composite decking in Houston? expand_more

Yes — we soft-wash composite decking (Trex, TimberTech, Fiberon) at low pressure using a manufacturer-safe biodegradable cleaner. High pressure would strip the surface texture and void the warranty.

How often should I wash my deck in Houston? expand_more

Most Houston decks need washing every 12 months. Covered or shaded decks accumulate mildew faster. Wood decks not resealed after cleaning may need washing every 6 months.

Do you offer deck sealing after washing? expand_more

Yes — we offer wood brightener and sealing as add-on services after the initial deck wash. This protects the wood grain and extends the time before the next cleaning is needed.
