Local to Pearland

From Old Pearland to Shadow Creek Ranch.

Pearland grew fast. The original "Pearland" along Broadway and Main is full of homes from the 60s, 70s, and 80s with painted siding, weathered brick, and concrete that's seen decades of Gulf Coast humidity. Then there are the newer planned communities — Shadow Creek Ranch, Silverlake, Southfork, Pomona — built in the last 20 years with Hardie siding, premium asphalt shingles, and stamped-concrete driveways.

The same pressure washer that's perfect for Shadow Creek's newer concrete will strip paint off a 1970s Pearland home. That's why PrimeStar Wash always inspects first, then matches method to surface: soft wash for painted and aged surfaces, surface-cleaner high pressure for solid concrete, and a careful combination for everything in between.

We dispatch from Houston via 288 (about 25 minutes). Free quote in 24 hours, no markup, no upsells.
