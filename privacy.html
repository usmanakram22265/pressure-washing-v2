Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

PrimeStar Wash ("we," "us," or "our") operates primestarwash.com. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide voluntarily, including:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, and email address submitted through our quote form or when calling us directly.
  • Service request details — property address, service type, and any notes you include in the quote form.

We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral advertising scripts, or third-party analytics on this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request by phone or email within 24 hours.
  • To schedule and complete the services you request.
  • To send invoices and follow up after service if needed.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain customer contact and service records for up to 3 years for business accounting purposes, after which they are securely deleted.
