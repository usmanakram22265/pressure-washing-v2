Driveway Sealing in Sugar Land

Why Sugar Land homes choose PrimeStar for driveway sealing.

Sugar Land's concrete driveways expand and contract with Fort Bend County's clay soil — sometimes dramatically during wet-to-dry cycles — producing hairline cracks and surface crazing that accelerate if left untreated. Once water enters small cracks, Houston's subtropical heat cycle turns it into freeze-thaw damage in rare cold snaps and persistent crumbling from below in normal years. Sealing after a professional cleaning is the most cost-effective way to extend driveway life and prevent expensive slab repairs.

We apply a penetrating concrete sealer (not a surface-coat film sealer) after a thorough surface clean and a minimum 24-hour dry time. Penetrating sealers bond with the concrete matrix and resist oil, water, and UV fading without leaving a glossy film that becomes slippery when wet. For driveways with existing cracks, we offer crack chasing and polyurethane fill prior to sealing.

Scheduling driveway cleaning and sealing as a single appointment in Sugar Land saves 10% off the combined job and reduces mobilization time. We dispatch via the US-59/Southwest Freeway corridor — most addresses are within 20 minutes. Free 24-hour written quote on both services.
