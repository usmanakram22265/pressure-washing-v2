What Affects Your Houston Pressure Washing Quote

Last updated: May 2026 · By Uzi Arslan, Owner — PrimeStar Wash

Houston homeowners often wonder why two pressure washing quotes for similar-looking homes can be very different. The answer is that several variables combine to determine the actual work involved — and a thorough quote reflects those variables accurately. Here's what professional exterior cleaning companies in Houston factor into every estimate.

How to get an accurate quote: Request a free quote online or call 832-329-2641. We assess your specific surfaces, staining level, and home size and return a written estimate within 24 hours — with no obligation to accept and no deposit required.

Surface Type and Material

Different surfaces require different techniques, detergents, and time. Asphalt shingles require a low-pressure soft wash with sodium hypochlorite — a slow, careful process. Concrete driveways can be cleaned with high-pressure rotary equipment faster. Composite decking needs low pressure and specific biodegradable detergent to avoid warranty damage. Painted wood trim and Hardy board require lower pressure than unpainted brick. A job with multiple surface types involves multiple technique changes, which affects the time on-site.

Home Size and Number of Stories

Square footage is the most straightforward variable — larger driveways, longer fences, and bigger roofs simply take more time and materials. Stories add complexity in a non-linear way: two-story work involves ladders, extended equipment, and sometimes safety gear that single-story work doesn't. A large two-story home on a slab with a steep roof pitch is a fundamentally different job than a single-story ranch, even if both are listed as "3 bedrooms."

Staining Level and Time Since Last Cleaning

A surface last professionally cleaned 12–18 months ago in good condition requires less dwell time, fewer passes, and less detergent than one that hasn't been touched in 4–5 years. Heavy black algae colonization on a roof requires a longer sodium hypochlorite dwell period. Oil stains on driveways that have been there for years require pre-treatment with industrial-grade degreasers before the surface wash. Condition at arrival is one of the biggest drivers of time-on-site for any job.

Access and Site Conditions

Easy access to a wide-open driveway with a centrally located spigot and no obstacles is the baseline. Jobs become more complex when there are pool fences to work around, close neighbor properties on both sides of the fence, gate codes and limited access windows, or limited water pressure from an aging spigot. These aren't reasons to decline a job — they're reasons for a more accurate quote that reflects the actual setup.

Service Scope: Single vs. Combined

A quote for house washing alone is different from a quote for house washing plus roof soft wash plus driveway cleaning in the same appointment. When multiple services are scheduled together, the total cost per service is lower — mobilization costs are shared, and the crew is already on-site. Bundle pricing is one of the most consistent ways Houston homeowners reduce their per-service cost over time.

Red Flags in a Quote

  • Unusually low "whole-house" flat rate. Quotes without any assessment of your specific surfaces typically involve corner-cutting: no soft wash, no insurance, no pre-rinse of landscaping. The risk of damage to siding, trim, or roofing is real.
  • Quote given with no questions asked. A legitimate quote requires knowing your home's size, stories, roof type, and surface materials. A text-message quote with no inspection is often the basis for a later upcharge.
  • Cash-only with no receipt. This is the marker of an uninsured operator. Any damage to your property is your problem, not theirs.
  • Large upfront deposit required. Standard practice for residential Houston pressure washing is payment on completion. No legitimate company requires a large deposit for a residential cleaning job.

What a Fair Quote Includes

A written quote from PrimeStar Wash includes:

  • Specific surfaces to be cleaned, listed individually
  • Technique used per surface (pressure wash, soft wash, or both)
  • Pre-rinse and post-rinse of all landscaping as standard
  • Proof of general liability insurance available on request before the job
  • Payment due on completion — no deposit required

Bottom line: The only accurate quote is one based on your specific home. Request a free quote online and we'll return a written estimate within 24 hours — no pressure, no deposit, no obligation.
